Arndroid Notifications

Arndroid Notifications is a private, non-commercial text message program operated by Matt Perser, an individual. It sends short call notes, occasional priority alerts, and reminders a recipient has asked for, to two recipients who have agreed to receive them. There is no public sign-up and no marketing.
