Privacy Policy

Arndroid Notifications is a private, non-commercial text message program operated by Matt Perser, an individual. It sends short call notes, occasional priority alerts, and reminders that a recipient has asked for. It has two recipients: the operator and one personal contact who has agreed to receive messages. There is no public sign-up and no marketing.

Information we collect

The mobile phone number of each recipient, the date each recipient gave consent, and the content of the messages sent.

How we use it

Only to send the messages described above and to keep a record of consent. We do not use it for advertising or marketing.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Messages are transmitted through our messaging platform and mobile carriers solely so they can be delivered.

Text message terms

Message frequency varies; typically a few messages per week. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to cancel. Reply HELP for help, or email info@ditchhook.com. See the Terms and Conditions.

Retention and deletion

Information is kept while a recipient remains in the program. A recipient can ask for their number and consent record to be deleted at any time by emailing info@ditchhook.com.
