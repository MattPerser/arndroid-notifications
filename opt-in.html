How to join

Arndroid Notifications has no web form and no keyword sign-up. It has two recipients, and each joined in the way described here.

1. The operator

The operator, Matt Perser, enrolled his own mobile number.

2. One personal contact, by verbal consent

Before any message is sent, the operator reads the following to the contact, in person or by phone:

"Do you agree to receive text messages from Arndroid Notifications, operated by Matt Perser, at this mobile number? You will get short call notes and reminders you ask for, a few messages per week. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to cancel, or HELP for help. Agreeing is not a condition of buying anything or of using the phone line. The terms and privacy policy are at this site's Terms and Conditions page and Privacy Policy page."

If the contact answers yes, the operator records the date of consent and adds the number by hand. If the contact answers no, no messages are sent.

After joining

Message frequency varies; typically a few messages per week. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time, or HELP for help. See the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
